{% icon "help" 16 %} How organizations work
  • Each organization is a separate tenant: its own documents, vector collection, chats, members and API keys. Nothing is shared between them.
  • Success is the share of answers in the last 30 days that weren't an AI error. Failed counts those errors.
  • Suspending blocks the organization's members and API keys immediately, without deleting anything. Activate undoes it.
  • Open an organization to manage its members, documents, keys and limits, or to delete it.
