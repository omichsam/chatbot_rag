{% icon "help" 16 %} How plans work
  • Pick a plan on an organization's page. It sets the monthly question limit right away; the document and member caps are checked when admins upload or add people.
  • Cloud AI providers allowed off: the organization only uses local providers (Ollama), then quoting, whatever its own settings say.
  • An organization's own limit can still be edited; applying the plan again resets it.
  • A plan in use can't be removed. Move its organizations to another plan first.
