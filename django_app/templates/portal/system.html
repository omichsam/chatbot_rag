{% icon "help" 16 %} How the parts fit together
  • Database stores organizations, users, documents list, chats, ratings and keys.
  • Vector store holds each document's passages as number-vectors, one collection per organization, so searches can't cross tenants.
  • Embeddings turn text into those vectors with a local model on this server; no data leaves for this step.
  • AI answers are written from the top matching passages by the providers on the AI providers page, tried in order. If they all fail, answers quote the documents instead.
  • Indexing processes uploads in the background: read, split into chunks, embed, store.
  • Test buttons run a real call and record the result in the audit log. The AI test calls the first enabled provider and uses a tiny amount of its quota.
