{% icon "help" 16 %} How user controls work
  • A user account exists once on the platform and can belong to several organizations, each with its own role (owner, admin, member).
  • Reset password sets a random temporary password and shows it to you once. The old password stops working immediately.
  • Deactivate blocks sign-in everywhere without deleting anything. Their questions and documents stay.
  • Superadmin (console owner) gives full access to this console and Django's /admin/, across every organization. Keep it to one or two people; changing it asks you to type the username.
  • Console role gives narrower access without superadmin: Support can view organizations, users, health and the audit log, and use read-only support mode; Finance can view usage and plans.
  • 2FA shows who has set up an authenticator app. Reset 2FA removes it so they can set it up again (e.g. after losing their phone).
