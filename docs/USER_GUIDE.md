# User guide

DocAssist answers questions using your organization's documents. Every answer shows the file and page it came from, so you can check it.

## Asking a question

1. Open **Chat** in the menu, or press **New chat**.
2. Type your question and press Enter. On a new chat you can also click one of the suggested questions.
3. The answer appears word by word. Under it you'll see the **sources**: the file and page each part came from (if your organization shows them).

Tips:

- Ask in your own words. DocAssist searches by meaning, so "how do I protect my invention?" finds passages about patents.
- Follow-up questions work: "what about the second one?" uses the last few messages for context.
- If the documents don't cover something, DocAssist says so instead of guessing, and your question is passed to your admins as a *knowledge gap* so they can add the missing information.
- Answers may be marked *Quoted directly from your documents*. That means the AI writer was unavailable, so DocAssist showed the most relevant sentences instead. They're still accurate; they just aren't reworded.

You can also ask from anywhere: type in **Ask your documents anything…** at the top of the page, or press **Ctrl+K**, type a question and press Enter.

## After an answer

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| 👍 / 👎 | Rate the answer. A 👎 lets you add a comment; admins see it under *Feedback*. |
| Save | Keeps the answer under **Saved answers**, only visible to you. |
| Copy | Copies the answer text. |

## Your history

- **My history** lists your past chats, grouped by date. Open one to continue it, or delete it.
- **Saved answers** lists the answers you saved, newest first, with their sources.
- **Ctrl+K** also searches your past questions and the documents.

## The Home page

**Home** shows how the assistant is being used: questions asked, how many were answered from the documents, top topics, the most-used documents and your recent chats. Use the 7 / 30 / 90 days switch to change the period.

## Your account

- **Change password, dark mode and log out** are in the menu under your name (bottom left).
- **Customize menu** lets you reorder, pin or hide menu items. It only affects your own view.
- **Several organizations:** if you belong to more than one, switch between them with the organization name at the top left. Each one has its own documents and chats.
- **Need admin tools?** Members see an *Admin tools* card listing what admins can do. **Request access** sends a request to your organization's admins.

## Long lists

Tables and lists show 10 items at a time. Use the search box above a table to filter it, click a column heading to sort, and use the page numbers below to move through the rest.
